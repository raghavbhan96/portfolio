How I work with AI

I treat AI as a tool in a delivery process, not a replacement for one. I own the problem, the constraints and the sign-off. The tools draft, extract and write code faster than I could alone.

  1. [01] Scope

    My decisions
    Pick a problem with a measurable cost, define what "good enough" means, and decide where a human must stay in the loop.
    What the tools do
    Help me explore approaches and spot edge cases in sample data.

  2. [02] Build

    My decisions
    Architecture, data handling, output format, and what is never sent to a model, such as client-confidential data.
    What the tools do
    Write most first-draft code and prompts, which I read, run and revise.

  3. [03] Validate

    My decisions
    Pilot with the people who will use it (PMs and leadership), fix what they flag, and only then replace the old process.
    What the tools do
    Nothing ships on model output alone. Outputs are reviewed before they reach users, and the time and dollar figures above come from real usage, not tool estimates.
